MakeUp Ultra Fast 9.5e — Javier Garduño — GNU LGPL version 3.

The supplied OPEN_ME.html contains a WebGL adaptation of MakeUp's tone curve,
color palettes, stochastic shadows, bloom, depth-based ambient occlusion and
exposure. This game adapts that supplied source to the pinned Three.js engine.
It is not the complete Minecraft shader pipeline and is not a Psyonix shader.

Changes, 2026-09-13:
- Host camera, moving model transforms, world units and shadow texture bindings.
- Preset-dependent pass budgets and a single-sampled scene for heat refraction.
- Live graphics editor, per-car finishes and preserved map color compensation.

The editable corresponding adaptation source is shipped in:
src/materials/reference-car-fragment.js
src/materials/reference-scenery.js
src/rendering/reference-post-shaders.js
src/rendering/reference-post.js
src/settings/reference-palettes.js
